How to Play

Select a Difficulty:

Choose your desired difficulty level from the dropdown menu.

Start the Game:

Click the "Play" button to begin.

Flip Boxes:

Click on the boxes to reveal the colours. Try to remember the positions of the colours.

Match Pairs:

Try to find matching pairs of colours. If you find a match, they will remain flipped; if not, they will flip back after a short delay.

Track Your Progress:

Keep an eye on the score and the timer displayed on the screen.

Game Over:

Once all pairs are found, the game will display a "GAME OVER" message, and you can start a new game by clicking the button provided.
